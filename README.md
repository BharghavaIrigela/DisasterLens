# DisasterLens

### AI-Powered Disaster Damage Intelligence & Response Prioritization

DisasterLens is an AI-powered disaster intelligence system designed to analyze pre-disaster and post-disaster satellite imagery to detect infrastructure damage, estimate damage severity, and generate actionable intelligence for emergency response.

## Problem

After natural disasters such as floods, earthquakes, cyclones, and wildfires, emergency responders need to quickly determine which areas have been affected and where limited resources should be deployed first.

Traditional damage assessment can be slow, manual, and difficult to scale across large affected regions.

## Proposed Solution

DisasterLens aims to transform satellite imagery into actionable disaster-response intelligence through:

- Pre- and post-disaster image analysis
- Automated change detection
- Infrastructure damage detection
- Damage severity estimation
- Geospatial damage mapping
- AI-assisted response prioritization
- Explainable damage assessment

## Key Objectives

1. Detect changes between pre-disaster and post-disaster imagery.
2. Identify damaged infrastructure and affected regions.
3. Estimate the severity of detected damage.
4. Prioritize affected areas for emergency assessment.
5. Provide interpretable evidence supporting the generated priorities.
